LEGA6 wants this site to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to follow the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations 2013 and Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, as far as possible.

  • A “skip to content” link at the top of each page
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Respect for the device’s “reduce motion” setting
  • This menu: text size, contrast, inverted colours, grayscale, highlighted links, readable font, big cursor, text spacing and stopping motion
