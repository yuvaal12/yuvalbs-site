Accessibility statement

I believe it is important that this site be accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. The site was built in line with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 at level AA, as far as possible.

What’s been done

  • Full keyboard navigation support and a clear focus indicator
  • A link "Skip to content" at the top of the page
  • Alt text for images
  • Respects the device’s "reduced motion" setting
  • An adjustments menu: text size, contrast, grayscale, highlighted links, readable font, large cursor, spacing and stopping animations

What’s not perfect yet

Some portfolio images contain text inside the image, and some videos are visual only. I keep improving.
